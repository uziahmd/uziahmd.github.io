## The decision

**Structure: one page, one scroll, one URL — ruthlessly distilled, with the publication record moved to band one.** The page is not long because it has six sections; it is long because it says the same things twice and puts the payload last: About paraphrases the three entries beneath it (including a character-identical lift of "led its personalization component" from `index.html:670`), both tag rows repeat the Stack plate, eight track paragraphs re-describe five papers the record describes again, and 1,080px of fixed section padding runs across eight bands before a word is set. **World: Timebase is kept and refined, not replaced** — the critique's verdict is that the visual tells are already beaten (no Inter, no gradient hero, no glass, no soft-shadow cards, no 01/02 numbering, no fade-up reveals, no near-black-plus-neon dark) and that what gives the page away is linguistic and structural: a shipped design log, live spec documents, seven coordinated devices used once each, and legends that narrate the notation. So the refinement is subtractive on the doctrine and corrective on the build: **seven devices become three, and one of the three — the measurement strip — is repeated everywhere until it reads as handwriting rather than as a kit**, while **two places are mandated where a fact breaks the grammar with no caption**, because a record with no seams reads as generated. Nothing is added that does not already exist in today's stylesheet, and where a fact is missing the section shrinks.

**On tabs: no — and the reason is mechanical, not aesthetic.** `:target` is a document singleton and the measurement cursor already owns it (`.record li:target`, `index.html:368-378` — seven occurrences across five lines, all `li:target`), so a CSS-only tab switcher cannot coexist with your one signature interaction, and a JS switcher that hides panels makes a chip target an unrendered row: the click silently does nothing. Tabs would also put a click between a PI who was mailed `uziahmd.github.io/#pub-emnlp26` and the six rows she came for, and would break find-in-page, print and no-JS. Every lens that examined the record — recruiter, PI, IA, copy — argued against tabbing Publications, and the only defensible split anyone found was the reverse (Projects/Toolkit behind routes, ~2,300px). After this cut those bands are worth about 1,400px on a 5.4-screen page: **the distillation makes tabs unnecessary rather than impossible.** The safe form, if you still want it after seeing the shortened page, is spelled out in full in Open Decision 5.

## Effort and risk

**Effort: 15–18 focused hours across five sittings, plus about 30 minutes of the owner's time.** Phase 1 is **45 minutes**, is pure deletion, carries zero visual and zero contract risk, and should ship today whatever is decided about the rest. Phase 2 is the longest at 5–6 hours: the fact ledger must be executed row by row, moving six headings into the rail touches every section's layout, and the track flattening repeats six times; expect to rebuild the responsive block at the end rather than patching as you go. Phase 3 is 3 hours, most of it the two-column hero and the thirty-cell perch sweep, which is a measurement task and not a coding one. Phase 4 is 3–4 hours of careful copy work with the résumé open — sequence it after the owner session. Phase 5 is 3–4 hours, of which the rem conversion is an hour best done as one before/after table. Every phase leaves a working, better page. No build step, no dependency, no framework.

**Risk 1, and the largest: the arithmetic does not reach the brief's target, and the numbers below are re-derived rather than promised.** The brief implies 40–50% of 1,431 words and about three desktop screens. Summing the section budgets against `metrics.json` heights — hero ~640 (more content, less padding), Publications 1,006 → ~1,070 (it *grows*, by five contribution clauses and two notes), Work 2,745 → ~1,500, Projects 1,264 → ~950, Stack+Recognition 1,058 → ~430 merged, footer 575 → ~300, About 555 → 0 — the honest floor with every main fact intact is **~870 words (61%) and ~4,900px (5.4 screens)**. Against the page's **non-citation prose** the cut is 1,191 → ~570, which is **52%**, and that is the number I would defend. Getting to 700 words or three screens means deleting facts. *(For calibration: the predecessor draft's ≤840 words / ≤3,700px targets were 15–20% optimistic against the same section heights; these are the corrected figures, and criterion 2 is set at ≤5,200px with headroom rather than at the estimate.)*

**Risk 2: the deep cut is subtraction, and subtraction is where a page goes generic.** About is gone, eight track paragraphs are gone, the close line, the labelled title block, the tag rows and the legends are gone. What remains carries personality on the strength of the record, the rail, the dash coding, two deliberate seams and one dragon. **If the result reads correct but cold, the diagnosis is that the page lost its only prose voice, and the remedy is not to restore About** — it is the ask line and the image, Open Decisions 1 and 3. **Risk 3: this is a heavy edit to the two things the Charizard contract actually measures** — the h1's immediate neighbourhood and the theme control — and both fail *silently*. A containing-block property un-fixes the dragon with no error; a hero geometry change puts him on the letters in the fraction of a second before the webfont swaps, which is the window nobody screenshots; and a wrapped name makes the Range union *shrink*, so the naive inequality passes while the perch is wrong.
