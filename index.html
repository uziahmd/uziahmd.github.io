About

I'm an AI researcher and engineer who takes systems from a research idea to something that runs — under real constraints, on real hardware, measured honestly. At KPST I shipped an infinite-stream industrial-safety vision-language model that watches never-ending camera feeds with bounded memory and emits schema-constrained hazard decisions to drive a live alarm system.

At KAIST's Knowledge & Democracy Lab I research LLM reasoning and difficulty perception, VLM bias and interpretability, and LLM safety: adversarial prefills, many-shot jailbreaking, model introspection. Earlier, at ICLab, I co-authored an IMWUT / Ubicomp 2024 reproducible mobile-sensing pipeline and led its personalization component. My strengths sit at the meeting point of PyTorch and Hugging Face fine-tuning, interpretability and evaluation-harness design, and reproducible experimentation.

Infinite-Memory Streaming VLM for Industrial Hazard Detection

KPST (Korea Platform Service Technology) · AI Researcher · Feb 2026 — Present

A real-time safety system that watches a continuous, never-ending camera stream and emits one compact, schema-constrained JSON hazard decision per step to drive a downstream alarm. No separate object detector: the VLM makes the call end to end.

  • Adapted Qwen2.5-VL-7B with StreamingVLM-style streaming attention (attention-sink + sliding-window KV cache, contiguous 3D RoPE) for constant memory and per-step latency regardless of stream duration. A camera can run for hours or days without KV growth or drift.
  • Designed a two-stage LoRA SFT pipeline (broad SFT, then event-centered annealing on hazard transitions) on a single V100 32 GB, freezing the vision tower.
  • Guaranteed 100% output validity via FSM / schema-constrained decoding, and measured generalization on held-out cameras never seen in training.

LLM Difficulty Perception working paper · in prep

Do LLMs perceive how hard a problem is, and does injecting more knowledge move that perception? Built the sandboxed I/O code-execution eval and the LoRA/QLoRA fine-tuning pipeline (TRL + PEFT) across 658 reasoning problems and 6 models.

LLM Safety & Introspection EMNLP 2026 · under review

Can a model tell that its own harmful reply was elicited by an adversarial prefill? Built the reproducible prefill-attack and introspection pipeline (ADVPrefix, Many-Shot Jailbreaking), and found that across 10 open-weight LLMs (3B–70B), none reliably recognizes its own compromised output.

VLM Ideology & Bias Localization

Extended political-ideology linear probing from LLMs to vision-language models over a ~550-politician multimodal dataset, then reused the probes to localize bias: attributing sensitive-attribute leakage to specific image regions and cross-modal tokens (Grad-CAM, Integrated Gradients, TCAV).

Reproducible Stress-Prediction Pipeline ACM IMWUT 2024

Co-authored the pipeline and led its personalization component: a multi-task TSGB learner that lifted AUROC by up to ~17% over the user-independent baseline on DeepStress.

CrossShift — Inter-Personal Differences ACM IMWUT 2026 · accepted

Quantified user-level data shift across 8 datasets / 693 users (PERMANOVA, Wasserstein / OTDD / JS divergences, LOSO), pinpointing concept shift P(Y|X) as the primary driver of cross-user degradation.

CausalCFF — Causal Analysis of Stress CHI EA 2025

Built a causal pipeline (POI clustering, association-rule mining, Convergent Cross Mapping) that surfaces high-lift behavior patterns and tests their causal impact on stress across six weeks of GPS, activity, and app-usage data.

Multimodal Sensor Integration

Fused audio, Aqara, Withings, and smartphone streams with KNN imputation and feature selection, identifying 60-minute windows and "Yesterday Epoch" features as the strongest configuration under LOSO / K-fold CV.
