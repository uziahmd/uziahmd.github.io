Infinite-Memory Streaming VLM for Industrial Hazard Detection

I built the model side: adapting the base VLM, its two-stage LoRA training, the constrained decoding, and the data and evaluation pipeline that scores it on held-out cameras.

One vision-language model reads a never-ending camera stream and emits a schema-constrained JSON hazard decision per step, straight into a live alarm: forklift entry and robot-zone intrusion, switched by prompt, no object detector in front of it.

  • Qwen2.5-VL-7B with StreamingVLM-style attention (attention sink, sliding-window KV cache, contiguous 3D RoPE); two-stage LoRA SFT on a single V100 32 GB with the vision tower frozen; FSM-constrained decoding over a compact JSON schema, so every decision parses.

CCTV Security: Real-Time Loitering, Intrusion and Fire Detection

I built a real-time CCTV pipeline for loitering and intrusion alarms: a D-FINE-L detector (DETR family) on TensorRT, OC-SORT tracking, zone rules and per-person state machines. I then took a fire and smoke detector from an aerial-footage baseline to a ground-level CCTV alarm in about five days: a D-FINE-L detector fine-tuned on leak-checked, site-split data, with tiled detection, a motion gate and a temporal event rule that gives one deterministic alarm per video.

  • Detected 58 of 60 intrusion and loitering events with 6 false alarms (1.14 per hour) on the 60 clips the rules were tuned on.
  • Averaged 11.6 ms per frame across the same 60 clips on an NVIDIA H200, and about 18.8 ms in a live demo on a shared H200, against a 33.3 ms budget.
  • Found and fixed a TensorRT miscompile that had cut confident-detection recall to 0.9191 against the reference runtime, restoring it to 1.0000.
  • Built a pre-registered video stress test with byte-reproducible replay and scoring: 2,169 test units across 25 camera conditions, run on the pipeline's own tuning clips.

Drone Fault-Diagnosis Desktop App for ETRI

I built a Windows and Ubuntu desktop app in which users define their own custom models and train them to detect anything they label in drone flight logs; the app is the infrastructure for that. It covers labeling ArduPilot logs, generating datasets, training in TensorFlow or PyTorch (LSTM, GRU, CNN-LSTM and BiLSTM classifiers are included), replaying flights with per-class predictions in 3D, and monitoring a live drone over MAVLink with RTSP video. I implemented design elements and specifications from a KPST designer and refined the app with ETRI's feedback.

  • After a live demo at ETRI on July 2, 2026, I delivered the app on August 4, 2026, with source, two trained models, datasets, Korean manuals and a walkthrough video.
  • A GPS-jamming CNN-LSTM trained through the app reached 99.5% per-window accuracy (100% recall, 98.4% precision) on held-out flight logs, scored against rule-derived labels.
  • Traced a cold-boot crash to a known Qt race; after a library upgrade, crashes under a synthetic repro fell from 3 of 10 launches to 0 of 20.
  • Wrote a one-command GPU/CPU installer; a headless end-to-end test on Windows passed 10 of 10 stages.

VLM Benchmark and Fine-Tuning for Factory-Safety Cameras

I tested and trained open vision-language models for two factory CCTV alarms: a worker inside a robot cell while the arm is moving, and a forklift entering through a gate. I built a zero-shot benchmark of 416 five-second windows and screened 15 model families for fit on one 32 GB GPU. I then built the fine-tuning stack (extended labeling tool, label audit, clip datasets, LoRA training, evaluation) and trained adapters for both alarms on a 9B open model.

  • The robot-cell adapter reached hazard F1 0.853 on 3,002 test clips from 10 held-out videos of the training camera, against 0.507 for flagging every clip.
  • A frame-difference label audit, with every flip reviewed by hand, corrected the labels on 6,885 robot-cell frames (about 3%).
